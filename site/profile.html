How fees reach you

Creator, protocol and referral earnings accrue in the vault and are pulled on demand. Nothing is ever pushed to you — on Arc a push-based split lets one blocked recipient break trading for everyone on that coin.

Creators earn 60–70% of the 1% trading fee for as long as the coin trades on its curve, then 70% of the pool's fees after it graduates. Referral shares apply to the launch phase only.
