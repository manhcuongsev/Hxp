Asset packs

What a buyer gets besides the ticker. Images only — PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF. You get 50 MB across the coin and spend it however suits: twenty small stickers or five large sprite sheets. No single file may be over 10 MB. Once the coin is created these are fixed — their fingerprint goes on-chain with everything else, so a later swap is visible rather than silent.

Curve

Every coin opens at a $2,000 market cap and graduates at $29,392, after $6,081 of buying. 79.31% of supply sells on the curve; the rest seeds the pool at the price the curve closed at. Priced in dollars, so those numbers do not move when anything else does.
Minimum 0.5 USDC, no maximum. Buying more of your own launch is allowed and is not hidden — the share you end up holding is published on the coin's page, and selling it early costs the same decaying tax as anyone else.
