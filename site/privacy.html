Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information Hexapus collects, how it is used, and what happens to it, when you visit hexapus.trade, connect a wallet, launch or trade a token, download an asset pack, or otherwise interact with the Hexapus interface (together, the "Platform").

The Platform is noncustodial. Hexapus does not control your wallet, hold your private keys or assets, or submit transactions for you. There are no accounts to create, and Hexapus does not ask for your name, email address, or telephone number.

2. Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Wallet and on-chain dataPublic wallet addresses, transaction hashes, token balances, trades, tokens you launched
Submitted contentToken names, symbols, stories, artwork, asset packs, social links
CommunicationsAnything you send when you contact us
Device and network dataIP address, browser type, and similar details present in ordinary server logs

Hexapus does not use analytics products, advertising pixels, session recording, or cross-site trackers, and sets no tracking cookies. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

A wallet address is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. It may be personal information where it can be associated with an identifiable person.

3. Sources of information

Information reaches Hexapus from three places: the public Arc blockchain, which Hexapus indexes; what you submit through the interface; and your browser, when it makes ordinary requests to the server.

4. How we use information

  • To display tokens, prices, holders, trades, and portfolios.
  • To serve artwork and asset packs to those a token's settings permit.
  • To verify, in memory and without storing it, that a signature proves ownership of an address before releasing a gated download.
  • To operate, secure, and diagnose faults in the Platform.
  • To respond to complaints and legal obligations.

Hexapus does not sell information and does not share it for advertising.

5. Blockchain information

Public ledgers are transparent by design. Wallet addresses, transactions, token activity, contract events, and token metadata are permanently readable by anyone, through Hexapus and through independent explorers and indexers. Hexapus cannot edit, hide, reverse, or delete information recorded on a public blockchain.

This includes a token's name, story, artwork reference, and asset-pack fingerprint, which are written to Arc when the token is created and are immutable afterwards.

6. How we disclose information

Hexapus discloses information to service providers that host the site and its data, and where required by law or to respond to a valid legal request. Anything published on-chain, or submitted as public token content, is disclosed to everyone by its nature.

7. Third-party services

Using the Platform means interacting with services Hexapus does not control:

  • Your wallet provider — its own policy applies to what it collects.
  • Arc RPC endpoints — they receive your IP address and your requests.
  • Circle Gateway, when moving USDC from another chain.
  • Hosting and CDN providers, which process requests to serve the site.

8. Browser storage

Your watchlist and a small number of interface preferences are stored by your browser and are never transmitted to Hexapus. Clearing your browser data removes them.

9. Retention

Server logs are kept only as long as needed to operate and secure the Platform. Submitted token content is retained for as long as the token exists, which in practice is indefinitely. On-chain data is retained by the network, not by Hexapus, and cannot be deleted.

10. Security

Hexapus takes reasonable measures to protect the information it holds. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and no guarantee of absolute security is given.

11. Content submitted by others

Artwork and asset packs are supplied by token creators, not by Hexapus. If material hosted here contains personal information about you, write to contact@hexapus.trade.

Hexapus can stop serving a file it hosts. It cannot remove that file from the blockchain, from anyone who has already downloaded it, or from anywhere else it has been copied.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you. Because Hexapus holds no account, no contact details, and no profile, most such requests have little to act on. Where Hexapus holds something and is able to act, it will.

Requests concerning blockchain records cannot be fulfilled. That limitation is technical and applies equally to Hexapus.

13. International processing

The Platform is reachable worldwide, and information may be processed in countries other than the one you are in, with different data protection laws.

14. Children

The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18, or under the age of majority where they live. Hexapus does not knowingly collect information from children.

15. Policy changes

This policy may be revised. The date at the top records when it last changed.
